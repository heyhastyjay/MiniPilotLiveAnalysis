post processing

Carbon balance. Feed carbon is the inlet at reactor exit (first inlet meter, Module5 Inp2) times the 33.33% CO in the syngas. Product carbon is the Cole-Parmer mass flow times the GC carbon (CO+CO2+CH4). Gas that left through a bypass or a blowdown was not measured by the product meter, and there is no vent total to put back into the balance. Those injections are hidden. They are not given a corrected conversion.

The first inlet meter was used to pressurize the system with a manual bypass, so that gas is in the inlet total and not in the product meter.
- 10:43–11:20. Inlet about 1000 sccm, reactor still 20°C, pressure hunting between 0 and 376 psi (setpoint 200–250). Injections 001–005.
- 14:06–14:16. Setpoint moved from 250 psi to 700 psi. Inlet stayed near 300 sccm. The product meter read zero from 14:06, then a slug of 1.4 SLPM at 14:15 as the bypass came off. Pressure did not rise smoothly. It peaked and relieved: 685 psi at 14:07:50, 810 at 14:08:30, 866 at 14:10:00, 790 at 14:11:00, 819 at 14:12:20, 810 at 14:13:10, 803 at 14:14:00, and held near 710 psi by 14:17. Injections 019 and 020.

Blowdowns in the SpecView pressure log (26_324.LOG), and what they do to the balance:
- 12:32:40–12:36. Pressure 255 → 909 psi, then down to about 269 psi, setpoint still 250. Inlet had just been set to 300 sccm. The product meter log starts at 12:40, so this vent did not pass the meter. Injection 011 left the reactor at 12:36:48, in the tail of that vent.
- 14:07–14:16. The relief spikes listed above, same interval as the bypass pressurization. Each drop is gas that left through the relief rather than the product meter.
- 20:21:50–20:26. Pressure 715 → 129 psi, inlet already at zero, setpoint still 700. The last parcel left the reactor at 20:10 (injection 052). This blowdown is after every injection, so none is hidden for it, and the balance is not extended past 052.

No other blowdown between 14:17 and 20:21. Pressure stayed near 710–735 psi. The 16:20 and 19:30 moves are the flow steps to 800 sccm (about 20 psi), not vents.
